Flow appearance

Appearance in the sidebar opens a compact background chooser. Clicking a palette or finish previews it; hovering only enlarges the thumbnail. Save choice persists a versioned preference for the selected broker in this browser. Cancel, Escape, the close button and backdrop clicks restore the latest saved preference. Selecting Use original Flow background restores the original canvas. A missing, invalid or unavailable preference uses the original background. Storage failures are visible and never reported as saved.

Six palettes coordinate the workspace background, cards, navigation, controls and action highlights. Classic pairs warm neutrals with teal; Gallery White pairs the selected low-contrast natural gray/white marble sidebar (35% desaturation, nine soft branching veins) with white cards and cool silver controls, plus restrained static glass gradients and limited 8px backdrop blur; Elm combines an original Woodland Mist sidebar with Silver Birch’s warm ivory/sage background, subtle upper-right elm foliage, cream surfaces, sage selections and forest-green controls; its former purple tones are removed; Pastel Bloom softens its lavender background with a warm ivory/faint sage transition beside the sidebar, cream at the bottom and gentler architectural shapes and pairs Cloud Tint (#F6F8FC) marble cards with Sky Mist (#DCEAF7) selections, Moonstone Blue (#B8C8D8) recommendation boxes, a warm ivory watercolor wildflower sidebar, sage navigation selections and eucalyptus-green (#426451) action accents; Coastal pairs its existing aqua canvas and cream cards with an original sea-glass sidebar, readable pale mint navigation, frosted mint/aqua controls and deep teal labels; Midnight pairs a light-charcoal (#626970) gunmetal marble sidebar with Cloud Tint (#F6F8FC) cards, pale blue-white (#E3EAF4) selections/recommendations and cool silver (#D8E2EB) controls on a cool mist canvas that blends smoky silver beside the sidebar into muted slate, with a pearl-gray lower wash and quieter architectural shapes. Midnight links and small labels use dark ink for readability on white; its progress bars use charcoal for visible contrast. Three static finishes control the background texture independently. Urgency, errors, success and destructive actions keep their semantic colors. Original Flow restores the existing styling. Backgrounds use static CSS. Elm bundles a Woodland Mist sidebar and three distinct workspace JPEGs: Architectural uses ivory/sage planes, fine vertical grain and soft branch shadows; Botanical uses recognizable elm foliage around the edges; Atmospheric retains the existing faint upper-right foliage background unchanged. All are static and add no external image calls, animation or backdrop filtering. Coastal includes one bundled original sea-glass JPEG texture, with no external image calls, animation or backdrop filtering; navigation and footer use readable pale mint backing. Pastel Bloom includes one bundled watercolor JPEG sidebar texture; ivory backing keeps navigation and footer labels readable over the artwork. It adds no animation, external image calls or backdrop filters. Gallery White includes one bundled 23 KB SVG sidebar texture, with no external calls, animation or new dependencies; backdrop blur is limited to a few visible surfaces, not long lists. Reduced-motion preferences suppress the small hover enlargement. The chooser is keyboard accessible and uses a native modal dialog.

Storage key: vesta.flow.appearance.v1.broker.<brokerId>. Preference storage is local to this browser and origin, not synced across devices. Broker changes close any open preview and load that broker's saved preference. Backgrounds never write to FUB, Render, action history, cadence clocks or appointment drafts. React background updates do not remount the workspace.
