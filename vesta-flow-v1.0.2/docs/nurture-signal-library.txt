Vesta nurture signal and message library
Version nurture-evidence-library-2026-11-21-v1
STATUS Review only. Not imported into the live AppointmentMessages engine.

Scope
60 original OUT techniques, 180 original A/B/C approaches, 540 original message
cores, 1,080 SMS/email draft records. Channel adaptations share the core; hooks
are additional fact-bound personalization and are not counted as new strategies.
No sender, collector, CRM writes, service, fees, cadence or queue modifications.

Authoritative sources
The November 5 Vesta Personalized Outreach Architecture Playbook provides the
OUT IDs, original approach labels and selection/evidence rules. The source is
not an exhaustive ontology or an implemented API. Departments/SOP guidance and
Peter's later instructions retain control of current timing, consent, ownership,
appointment verification and integration evidence. Older third-party example
cadences do not override Vesta's rules.

Signal interpretation
NUR.* is a stable detector/interpretation code; OUT-* is the outreach purpose;
OUT-*-A/B/C is the approach. Each message also has phase and channel identifiers.
A signal needs exact person ID, evidence IDs, original eventAt, interpretation
observedAt, explicit validUntil, verified condition status and reviewed approach
IDs. The requiredFacts list identifies what the trusted adapter must provide.
The library does NOT turn generic Smart List labels or keywords into verified
customer intent. No automatic FUB/Zenlist raw-data adapters are activated here.
A broker or future tested evidence interpreter reviews approach applicability
against the original selection rule. Missing facts are held, not invented.

Conditions and selection
Unknown / waiting / approaching / met / changed / withdrawn / contradicted are
separate states. Withdrawn and contradicted cannot generate outreach. Customer
pauses, identity conflicts, incomplete communication coverage, unverified owners,
wrong sender, contact restrictions and excluded stages hold all proposals.
Dates on historical evidence are retained; interpretation freshness is explicit.
Candidates rank direct inquiries, verified requested information and due promises
above general opportunity review. The existing cadence must independently allow
the action; the library sets no new intervals. Pending replies only permit an
inquiry with the exact pending event timestamp. Secondary signals stay context.
Final comparison with production orchestration remains required before wiring.

Follow-ups and repetition
Opening, follow-up and alternate-angle are choices, not a 3-touch campaign.
Each follow-up requires an actual prior send for that approach/signal plus a fresh
cadence-due decision. Proposed/held drafts never establish a send. Any customer
reply requires a new review; a newly agreed pause overrides the sequence.
History includes proposed, edited, held and sent records. contentId spans SMS/email
so switching channel or greeting cannot recycle the same core. Sent signal keys
prevent re-opening the same event. Exhaustion holds instead of fabricating more
copy. The helper has no persistence: production MUST supply the complete shared
customer history and use atomic reservation/receipts before simultaneous brokers
can share this safely. Do not claim global deduplication from this local helper.

History hooks
Only a verified, connected personal call or personal message exchange with this
sender, exact person and source ID can provide a dated hook. Generic notes,
imports, assignment dates, automated notices and call attempts cannot. The date
is rendered with month AND year in the review context's declared timezone. A
message exchange is called messages; a call can be called a conversation. A new
broker gets an honest introduction, never a fake "we spoke". Only reviewed
customer-safe facts may fill slots; source text is data, not instructions.
No conversation evidence means no history hook. All claims retain evidence IDs.

Reply handling
Interested: clarify the actual need, offer the relevant focused appointment and
verify availability before confirming. A question: answer or assign a department
deliverable first. Not now: save the condition/date. Changed: reselect. Stop: stop.
Silence: retain existing cadence, no negative customer label or broker penalty.
A useful answer or fulfilled commitment can be the next step before a meeting.

Learning archive and promotion
Archive editable document, PDF, complete JSON and browser review together under
one version. Review comments reference exact messageId or OUT approach ID. Record
old wording, changed wording, evidence/case reference, reason, reviewer/date and
observed outcome. Broker acceptance/edits are not proof of conversion. Evaluate
comparable populations; preserve held and negative outcomes. No automatic model
training or policy promotion. An approved catalog revision gets a new version;
prior drafts/history retain the version actually used. Document edits do not
silently update production. Candidate strategy additions remain review-only.

Before production integration
Map existing trusted evidence into these typed facts; prove original-time and
identity/owner/channel semantics with real contact comparisons. Connect shared
history/reservation, preserve edited/held drafts, recheck immediately before any
manual FUB handoff, and keep consent and current SOP in control. Deploy only with
Peter's standing production approval. This build does not request sender access.
