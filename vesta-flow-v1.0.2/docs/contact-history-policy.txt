Historical contact policy

Verified lifetime conversation history is separate from latest broker outreach,
latest customer response, intent and the active follow-up clock. Later attempts
can advance outreach/cadence without deleting an earlier conversation.

The source retains compact per-person proof from the existing calls/text mirror
pass: an explicitly outbound event before a valid inbound event, with evidence
IDs and original event times. Failed, automated, unknown-direction, future and
post-snapshot edits do not establish a pair. No message bodies are transported.
No vendor history rescan, FUB mutation or broker performance credit is added.

The shared TypeScript rule reads this identity-bound projection and all known
summary channels. Prior conversation / history needs review / no conversation
recorded remain distinct. An unpaired inbound event is a review signal, never
proof that a lead is untouched. Advanced actual CRM stages and verified connected
app outcomes remain valid; a derived nurture tag is not proof of conversation.

Structured Shilo consultation categories now count as documented contact. Free
prose, suggested or canceled consultations and generic sync notes do not. Parser
v2 forces a policy rebuild from retained evidence at the original snapshot cutoff.
Source timestamps are preserved, so policy compilation does not pretend to be a
fresh vendor pull. Raw CRM data and existing source/consumer isolation remain.

Flow, Cadence/reroute, the source manager compiler, the Flow target compiler, and
the independent Cadence routing compiler share the contract. Scaffolding and
proto carry the same lifetime rule without changing their original views.

Messaging updates dropdown history labels from the selected current FUB record.
Unmapped nurture remains planning work and does not authorize automatic drafts.
Appointment-set/met records require verification instead of another generic
appointment pitch. Newest-created ordering, manual FUB sending and the existing
Done sync workflow remain. No live sender or training promotion is enabled.
